# AI Spend Agent

AI Spend Agent is an AI tool from Realpage, part of the Lumina Workforce, that reviews approved invoices against vendor, contract, and budget rules a customer has set up. It flags possible exceptions and estimates savings so property teams can keep purchasing consistent across a portfolio.

## What it does

- Reviews approved invoice line items against preferred and compliant vendors by category, and flags catalog vendors sending offline invoices or working outside their provisioned properties
- Compares spend to customer-entered contract terms, pricing rules, and discounts to detect on- and off-contract purchasing
- Estimates potential savings using category assumptions the customer sets
- Tracks spend trends across properties and portfolios and highlights portfolio-wide savings opportunities
- Planned (not yet available): automated requisitions, approvals, and ordering, plus vendor recommendations, contracting help, and credentialing support

## Who uses it

Multifamily property teams and portfolio operators that want visibility into purchasing compliance and contract savings, using OpsTechnology or Spend Management in Accounting.
