# AI Resident Agent

The AI Resident Agent is an automated assistant that responds to residents around the clock by voice, chat, text, and email. It takes over routine questions, service requests, payment reminders, and renewal outreach so onsite staff have more time for retention-focused interactions.

## What it does

- Answers resident questions about amenities, policies, events, packages, payments, and renewals at any hour across several channels
- Creates work orders automatically, helps residents troubleshoot simple problems, and sends status updates
- Sends rent reminders and follows up on overdue or small balances to encourage on-time payment
- Sends personalized email and text renewal reminders at times the property chooses
- Provides audit logs, human escalation, and Fair Housing-aware, SOC 2 and privacy-aligned practices

## Who uses it

Multifamily property management teams that want to offload routine resident communication and reduce delinquencies while supporting retention.
