# AI Analytics Agent

The AI Analytics Agent is a conversational assistant built into Lumina Ascent that lets teams ask questions about market, asset, and portfolio performance in plain language. It answers with explanations, summaries, and visuals, and points to actions that could matter most.

## What it does

- Answers performance questions asked in everyday language, including follow-ups
- Diagnoses why a Signal is strong, weak, improving, or declining, and shows the drivers and supporting data
- Writes concise narrative summaries for portfolios, markets, and individual assets, suitable for leadership updates and reviews
- Builds, recreates, and modifies reports and visualizations from plain-language requests or user templates
- Recommends actions expected to have the greatest impact

## Who uses it

Executives, asset managers, and operators in multifamily who want to explore performance and agree on next steps without advanced analytics skills.
