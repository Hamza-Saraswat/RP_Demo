# AI Operations Agent

The AI Operations Agent is an automated assistant built into Realpage that handles recurring back-office property tasks such as lease and ledger checks, affordable file reviews, and move-out and renewal processing. It runs continuously and flags issues for staff to follow up on.

## What it does

- Compares leases against property management system ledgers to catch missed revenue and compliance risks, for both Conventional and Affordable portfolios
- Reviews affordable certifications and recertifications for errors, missing forms or signatures, and dual income files, then routes exceptions
- Presents staff tasks for renewals, transfers, and move-outs to help keep rent rolls accurate
- Detects unusual charges, adjustments, and billing inconsistencies, and answers staff questions about ledgers and payments
- Provides daily briefings, performance dashboards, and documented activity logs

## Who uses it

Onsite and property operations teams at multifamily properties, including conventional and affordable housing operators.
