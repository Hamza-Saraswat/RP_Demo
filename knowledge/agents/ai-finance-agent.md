# AI Finance Agent

The AI Finance Agent is an AI-driven assistant for multifamily finance teams that automates routine accounting tasks such as invoice handling, bank reconciliation and recurring expense tracking. It highlights exceptions and produces financial reports while passing items that need human judgment to staff.

## What it does

- Pulls data from invoices received by email or upload, suggests GL coding from past activity, and pre-fills invoices for review
- Links to banks or takes bulk statements, matches activity to GL transactions, and drafts journal entries for unmatched items
- Spots recurring vendors and expenses, flags missing invoices, and supports accruals and timely payment of critical vendors
- Reads contracts for key terms and dates, and automates tasks like approvals, escheatment and vendor maintenance
- Detects discrepancies and anomalies, and generates reports on financial activity, exceptions and trends

## Who uses it

Multifamily finance and accounting teams that manage high transaction volumes, compliance requirements and month-end close.
