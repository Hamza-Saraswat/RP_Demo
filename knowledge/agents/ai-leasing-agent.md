# AI Leasing Agent

The AI Leasing Agent is a digital assistant that handles prospect communication and guides renters through the leasing process for multifamily properties. It is one of the agents in the Lumina AI Workforce and connects to a property's management system for current unit information.

## What it does

- Replies to prospect inquiries around the clock by chat, text, voice, and email, and hands the conversation to a human leasing agent when needed
- Pulls live pricing, availability, and unit details from the property management system to answer questions about floor plans, amenities, and policies
- Schedules tours, sends confirmations and reminders, offers self-scheduling that syncs with team calendars, and follows up after tours
- Logs every interaction automatically in Knock CRM
- Provides audit logs, policy-based execution, human review, and bias monitoring, and shares context with other Lumina AI agents

## Who uses it

Multifamily property leasing teams and operators who want to respond to prospects faster and convert more leads into leases.
