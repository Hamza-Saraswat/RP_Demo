# AI Facilities Agent

AI Facilities Agent is an AI-driven maintenance assistant built into Realpage systems. It coordinates unit turns, technician tasks, vendors, and purchasing so multifamily teams can keep maintenance work moving with less manual effort.

## What it does

- Builds and sequences make-ready plans, prioritizing turns by move-ins and floor plan demand
- Assigns and rebalances technician tasks by skills, schedules, and capacity, with voice-based request capture and guided inspections in the field
- Schedules vendors, centralizes vendor communication, and hands off purchasing through Realpage Spend Management
- Tracks warranties, recalls, and asset history to inform repair-versus-replace decisions
- Provides daily briefings, exceptions, trends, and dashboards on maintenance performance

## Who uses it

Multifamily property operators and their maintenance and facilities teams, including technicians and managers who coordinate turns, service requests, and vendors.
