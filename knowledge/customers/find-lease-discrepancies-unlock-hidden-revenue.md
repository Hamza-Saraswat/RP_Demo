# Summit Property Management

Summit Property Management was spending days on manual lease audits that compared leases to ledgers, which risked missed discrepancies and slowed its multi-state expansion. It adopted the AI Operations Agent, which continuously checks leases against ledgers and flags discrepancies within its existing OneSite workflow.

## Results as published

- 789 audits completed within three weeks
- 131 hours of staff time saved
- Up to $110,000 in potential revenue uncovered across seven properties
- Audit times reduced from days to about 1.5 hours per property
