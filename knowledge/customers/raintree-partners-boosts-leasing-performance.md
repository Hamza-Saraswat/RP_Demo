# Raintree Partners

Raintree Partners moved its 45 communities off disconnected, siloed systems and onto a single front office platform, Knock CRM. The consolidation replaced inconsistent reporting, clunky tour scheduling, and duplicate data with a unified dashboard and automation.

## Results as published

- Saved leasing staff more than 3,500 hours per year
- Prospects self-schedule 70% of all tours
- Operations span 45 communities on one unified platform
