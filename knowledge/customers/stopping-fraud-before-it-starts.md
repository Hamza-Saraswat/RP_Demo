# Summit Property Management

Summit Property Management, which runs nearly 11,000 units, moved off a manual, basic identity verification setup that was letting through more fraudulent applications and driving up bad debt. It switched to a centralized, automated verification process with detailed risk scoring, which helped it catch fraud early in the leasing process and make faster decisions.

## Results as published

- 15% fraud detection rate at the initial stages of the leasing journey
- Average savings of $10,000–$15,000 per prevented fraudster
