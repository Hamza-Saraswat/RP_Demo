# Neighborly Ventures

Neighborly Ventures used G5 digital marketing services to promote a new lease-up community just outside Portland, Oregon. The aim was to establish an online presence quickly and turn leads into residents.

## Results as published

- Reached 145% of the move-in goal in the first 120 days
- Converted leads at 6.13% in the first three months, versus an industry average of 2.88%
- Increased click-through rate by 138%
