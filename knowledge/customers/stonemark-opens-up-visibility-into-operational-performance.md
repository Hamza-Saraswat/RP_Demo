# Stonemark

Stonemark, a multifamily property management company, adopted RealPage Knock CRM to get easier data aggregation, reporting, and real-time visibility into how its properties and employees perform as it acquired more properties. This helped it bring the management of a large portfolio under one system and convert prospects into residents more effectively.

## Results as published

- Consolidated management of 60+ properties across the Southeast and Midwest
- Occupancy rose by 20% for some properties
- Onboarding time for leasing agents cut to less than two weeks
