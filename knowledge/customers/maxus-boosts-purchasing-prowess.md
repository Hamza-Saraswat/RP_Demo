# Maxus Properties

Maxus Properties, a Kansas City-based owner and manager of mostly Class B apartment communities, rolled out RealPage Spend Management across its seven regions, one region per month, with on-site help from a RealPage representative. The tool now covers purchase orders, invoice processing, approvals, budget control, and vendor management, and new acquisitions can be added within days.

## Results as published

- Rollout completed across seven regions at a pace of one region per month
- Newly acquired properties can be brought into Spend Management within days, without significant upfront expenditures
- Portfolio of around 15,000 units across 15 states is covered by the purchasing process
- Faster order-to-invoice, tighter budget monitoring, and greater visibility and compliance across the communities (no figures given)
