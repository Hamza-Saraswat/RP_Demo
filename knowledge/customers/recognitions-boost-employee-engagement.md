# BH Management

BH Management, a large U.S. multifamily operator, adopted RealPage Recognitions to make employee recognition more consistent and to encourage peer-to-peer appreciation. Leaderboards and badges gave staff a reason to take extra courses and compete with one another.

## Results as published

- Over two-thirds of BH's 2,300+ employees were recognized during the beta launch
- Over 88% of employees have been recognized to date
- Nearly 40% of employees have recognized at least one other employee
