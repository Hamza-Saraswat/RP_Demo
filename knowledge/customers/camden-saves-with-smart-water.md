# Camden

Camden ran a Smart Water pilot with RealPage at two of its 171 properties to find leaks and cut water use. The pilot lowered consumption, produced savings and a quick return, and supported the company's ESG and sustainability goals.

## Results as published

- Overall water consumption fell by 9.5%, which is over 626,000 gallons
- $24,931 in annualized savings after the first three months
- ROI of over 300% in just three months
- 29% resident participation rate via the STRATIS mobile app
