# Goldrich Kest

Goldrich Kest had been juggling three systems and four applications, each with separate logins, for industry, safety and cybersecurity training, and tracking progress by hand. They moved to EasyLMS within the RealPage Unified Platform, which gave learners a single point of access and included ready-made content on professional and personal development as well as industry topics.

## Results as published

- In 2018, when the first phase was rolled out to on-site team members, monthly course completions were nearly 6X higher than the prior year on the old platform.
