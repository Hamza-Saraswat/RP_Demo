# Cambridge Management

Cambridge Management, which operates LIHTC, HUD, mixed-income, and rural development communities, replaced time-consuming manual lease and affordable file audits with an AI tool that automates the reviews within its OneSite workflow. The tool flags discrepancies early and coaches site staff, which strengthened compliance and freed up team time.

## Results as published

- 547 lease and affordable file audit tasks created in the first 90 days
- Improved consistency across 218 units
- More than 95 hours of manual review work saved for regional supervisors
- $285K in potential revenue identified
