# Carter-Haston

Carter-Haston, which manages 60 multifamily properties, wanted to cut the time leasing agents spent on property questions, reduce missed calls and incomplete prospect information, and turn more qualified leads into leases. It adopted an AI-driven omnichannel tool covering calls, texts, emails and chat.

## Results as published

- 7% increase in answered calls
- 165% increase in completed CRM contact records
- 211% increase in tour bookings
- 11 hours saved per leasing agent, per month, per property
