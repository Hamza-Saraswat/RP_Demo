# Champlain Housing Trust

Champlain Housing Trust, an affordable housing provider with 90 properties in northwest Vermont, replaced its resident management portal with RealPage LOFT to give residents easier self-service tools that work with its existing system. The change led to more residents paying online and less time spent by staff on user management.

## Results as published

- 33% increase in adoption of online payments by residents
- 1-2 hours per week saved by staff in user management
- Improved portfolio-wide communications
- Enhanced resident engagement
