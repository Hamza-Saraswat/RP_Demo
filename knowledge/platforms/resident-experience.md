# Resident Experience

Resident Experience is a platform that brings the resident's path through leasing, move-in, daily living, rent payment, and rewards into one connected journey, with the aim of reducing friction and helping keep residents longer. It includes an AI resident agent, a contact center, insurance, the LOFT resident portal, loyalty rewards, payments, and rent reporting.
