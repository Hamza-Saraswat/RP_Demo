# Online Leasing

Online Leasing is a platform that guides prospects from searching for an apartment through to signing a lease electronically. It reduces paperwork for leasing staff and works with all major property management systems.

## What it does

- Lets applicants register and apply online at any hour using custom-branded applications built from certified templates
- Verifies a prospect's government-issued ID from their mobile phone before a tour, returning a pass or fail result in seconds
- Combines screening data with configurable settings to help fill vacancies with qualified renters
- Creates and sends online lease renewals and supports eSignature so leases can be signed in minutes
- Accepts electronic payments by ACH, credit and debit card, and offers secure cloud storage for leasing documents

## Who uses it

Apartment operators and their leasing staff use it to handle applications, screening, lease signing and payments for prospects and renters.
