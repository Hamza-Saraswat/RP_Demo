# Lumina AI Workforce

Lumina AI Workforce is a coordinated group of seven specialized AI agents built for multifamily housing and embedded across the Realpage platform. The agents share context and automate workflows so property teams can offload routine tasks across the renter journey.

## What it does

- Handles prospect and resident communication, including answering questions, scheduling tours, payment reminders, service requests, and renewal support
- Supports on-site operations such as lease audits, move-ins, transfers, notices, and daily tasks
- Triages maintenance work orders, coordinates vendors, and helps speed up unit turns
- Reviews invoices and contracts, flags variances, and improves purchasing visibility and contract compliance
- Turns portfolio data into plain-language answers, summaries, and visual insights

## Who uses it

Multifamily property and portfolio teams, including leasing, resident services, operations, maintenance, finance, procurement, and analytics staff.
