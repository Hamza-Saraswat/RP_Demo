# Vendor Marketplace

Vendor Marketplace is a free network where property management companies can find and connect with vetted vendors across many service categories. It also lets users manage bidding, from sending requests to awarding contracts, in one place.

## What it does

- Lets users search for vendors by category, location, and other criteria
- Shows vendor profiles with bios, photos, videos, and certificates
- Surfaces vendors that meet compliance requirements to help lower risk
- Supports sending bid requests to selected vendors or posting public bid requests
- Collects bid responses and allows contracts to be awarded within the platform

## Who uses it

Property management companies and apartment communities looking for qualified service vendors, along with vendors who want to be found by them.
