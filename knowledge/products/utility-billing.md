# Utility Billing

Utility Billing is a resident billing and cost recovery service for multifamily properties. It produces utility statements, either on their own or combined with rent and other ledger charges, and links to property management software.

## What it does

- Sends residents electronic statements of their allocated utility charges, with options for utility-only or combined rent-and-utility bills
- Supports the common RUBS and submetering billing methods, including more than one method at a single property
- Connects with major property management systems to identify billable residents and upload accounts receivable charges
- Generates and processes final bills through automated processes or from within other property management software
- Offers payment processing of utility remittances through Realpage Payments, plus legal and regulatory monitoring and extended customer support

## Who uses it

Multifamily property owners and managers who want to recover utility costs from residents, and the residents who receive and pay the bills.
