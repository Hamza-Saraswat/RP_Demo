# Reputation Management

Reputation Management is a local SEO and online reputation monitoring platform that lets property teams oversee reviews, listings and social activity for all their properties in one dashboard. A managed option, Reputation Management Pro, has an expert team handle the work on the customer's behalf.

## What it does

- Monitors and lets teams respond to reviews on Facebook, Google, Yelp and ApartmentGuide.com, with response templates for single or bulk replies
- Uses sentiment analysis to automatically classify reviews as positive or negative
- Flags geo-tagged images and video that pose brand risk and promotes positive media to social profiles
- Gives a customizable Local Brand Visibility score based on reviews, social, listings, search ranking and competition
- Keeps property listings up to date through integration with Realpage Marketing Center

## Who uses it

Property managers and multifamily operators who want to manage online reputation across a property or portfolio to support lease conversion and resident retention.
