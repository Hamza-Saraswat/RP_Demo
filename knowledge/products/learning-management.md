# Learning Management

RealConnect is a learning management system for property management companies, built on the Adobe Learning Manager platform. It gathers Realpage product education, industry and compliance courses, and a customer's own training material in a single place.

## What it does

- Hosts Realpage product training, industry and compliance courses, and custom or live sessions in one platform
- Lets administrators build learning paths and certifications and assign training by role, property, team, or organization
- Automatically creates and updates user accounts using data from an HR system or other existing source
- Reports on learning progress, compliance and certification status, onboarding, and product adoption across individuals, teams, properties, and portfolios
- Offers mobile-friendly access so on-site staff can find and finish assigned training quickly

## Who uses it

Property management organizations, including on-site learners, their managers, and training administrators.
