# Prequalification

Prequalification is a Realpage offering that checks rental applicants for fraud before move-in by verifying who they are and how much they earn. It plugs into a property management system and aims to keep the leasing process smooth for legitimate applicants.

## What it does

- Verifies applicant income through bank login, payroll provider login, or AI-checked uploaded income documents
- Confirms identity using personal data checks, device and network checks, ID document checks, and selfie-based facial matching
- Uses machine learning and AI to spot fraud from risk signals across several data sources
- Gives onsite teams clear, visible reasoning behind approval decisions
- Works within an existing property management system to reduce bad debt from fraudulent renters

## Who uses it

Property management companies and onsite leasing teams at rental communities that want to screen out fraudulent applicants.
