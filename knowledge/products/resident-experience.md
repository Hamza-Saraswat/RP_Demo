# Resident Experience

Resident Experience is a group of solutions for multifamily operators built around the LOFT resident portal. It aims to keep residents engaged and retained, support staff efficiency, and open up ancillary revenue.

## What it does

- Brings rent payments, maintenance requests, real-time updates, communication and community engagement into one resident app (LOFT)
- Covers the resident journey from online leasing through renewal, including a marketplace for moving services, financial tools and a rewards program for online rent payments
- Integrates fully with the OneSite property management system
- Offers add-on services: insurance, a 24/7/365 maintenance contact center, security deposit alternatives, and credit rent reporting

## Who uses it

Multifamily property managers and operators who want to improve resident satisfaction and retention while cutting operating costs and adding revenue, along with the residents who use the app.
