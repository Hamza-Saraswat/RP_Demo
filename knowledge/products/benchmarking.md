# Benchmarking

Performance Analytics Benchmarking Pro is a RealPage analytics platform that gives multifamily owners and operators a view into how their portfolio is performing. It draws on lease-transaction data from the RealPage ecosystem to compare results against other properties.

## What it does

- Compares dozens of revenue, expense, and operational metrics against benchmarks
- Lets users benchmark performance both within their own portfolio and against external properties
- Supports customized reports, dashboards, and widgets built around chosen metrics
- Allows flexible selection of comparison groups and benchmarks
- Connects with major property management systems and lets users download lease-level details for a subject property

## Who uses it

Multifamily asset managers and portfolio owners or operators who want to see how their properties stack up in their market.
