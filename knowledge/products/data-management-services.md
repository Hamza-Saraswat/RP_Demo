# Data Management Services

Realpage Data Management Services (DMS) is a combined technology and managed-service offering that gathers, checks, and consolidates operational and financial data from a company's own systems, outside property managers, and venture partners. It uses AI and machine learning along with a dedicated team to deliver consistent, timely data for investment decisions.

## What it does

- Collects and aggregates data from multiple sources and disparate software systems
- Uses AI and machine learning to normalize, validate, and analyze incoming data
- Maps financial data from different operators' charts of accounts into a standard format for comparable reporting
- Applies hundreds of standard and configurable business-rule validations to catch inconsistencies
- Provides data quality dashboards tracking consistency, completeness, and timeliness, and supports onboarding and offboarding of acquired or sold assets

## Who uses it

Real estate investment owners and asset managers who need standardized data from third-party managers and partners across their portfolios.
