# Investor Management Services

Investor Management Services (IMS) is a cloud platform for commercial real estate firms, syndicators and fund managers to communicate with investors, run capital raises, and report on performance. It combines a CRM, investor dashboards, document delivery and automated distribution calculations in one place.

## What it does

- Provides a real estate-focused CRM to track investor contacts, engagement, communication history and investment activity
- Automates waterfall calculations, distributions and custom statements in place of spreadsheets
- Supports capital raises with pipeline tracking, online deal rooms, electronic signatures, commitments and capital call coordination
- Gives investors self-service dashboards, on-demand statements and shared documents such as K-1s
- Surfaces market overviews and property-level insights across a portfolio, including assets run by multiple third-party managers

## Who uses it

Private equity real estate firms, syndicators, and fund and portfolio managers who manage outside investors, along with the investors they serve.
