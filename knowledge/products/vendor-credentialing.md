# Vendor Credentialing

Vendor Credentialing is a service that checks and tracks whether vendors meet a property management company's insurance, licensing, and paperwork requirements before they work on a property. It automates document collection, monitoring, and renewal so approvals move faster and compliance risk drops.

## What it does

- Validates certificates of insurance, licenses, and W-9s against requirements set at the company level or by risk tier
- Monitors coverage and sends expiration and missing-item notices to vendors and their insurance agents
- Provides dashboards showing vendor compliance status, background checks, and risk levels
- Offers a vendor mobile app and an insurance agent portal for submitting documents and communicating in real time
- Awards a Credential Key to pre-vetted vendors so they qualify for same-day approval

## Who uses it

Property management companies that need to vet and monitor vendors, plus the vendors and their insurance agents who submit and maintain documentation.
