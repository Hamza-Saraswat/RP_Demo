# eSupply GPO

eSupply is a group purchasing program that links property operators with a network of national vendors who have already negotiated discounted pricing. Customers pick only the vendor categories they want, which cuts down the effort spent on sourcing and negotiating.

## What it does

- Provides access to pre-negotiated contracts with national vendors for upfront preferred pricing
- Lets customers choose vendors and categories to build a custom program
- Covers specialty vendor categories and shares product availability details
- Supplies industry news and information about vendor training
- Delivers quarterly reports on spend and savings, with account management support

## Who uses it

Property management and operations teams, such as procurement directors and operations managers, who buy supplies across their portfolios.
