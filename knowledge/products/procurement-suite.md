# Procurement Suite

Procurement Suite is a bundle of purchasing tools for rental real estate operators. It brings together spend control, group purchasing, vendor compliance, and vendor communication to help lower operating costs and improve visibility into what is being bought.

## What it does

- Streamlines purchasing, invoicing, and vendor activity to cut operating expenses
- Provides group purchasing access to discounted products through a supplier network
- Manages vendor credentialing to lower liability risk and support compliance
- Connects property managers and vendors across the procurement and invoice lifecycle
- Offers customized reporting and spend insights alongside hands-on services

## Who uses it

Property management companies in rental real estate that want to automate procurement and gain more control over spending and vendors.
