# Investment Accounting

RealPage Investment Accounting is a single accounting platform for organizations that manage real estate investment holdings, aimed at growing middle market firms. It covers consolidations, capital transactions, financial statements, and profit center tracking across several asset classes.

## What it does

- Handles partnership accounting, including capital calls, distributions, management fees, waterfalls, investor correspondence, reporting, and return tracking
- Handles corporate accounting by allocating overhead and expenses from the corporate entity to investments and properties, and reports on business units not tied to a specific asset
- Consolidates records across complex ownership structures and multiple subsidiaries, with automated rules to shorten the closing cycle
- Supports commercial, multifamily, hospitality, single family, senior living, and affordable housing investments
- Tracks and measures profit center performance

## Who uses it

Middle market real estate investment companies and other organizations that manage real estate investment holdings.
