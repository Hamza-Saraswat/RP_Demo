# Contact Center

Realpage Contact Center is a round-the-clock service that handles inbound prospect and resident communications for rental properties by phone, email, chat and text. It acts as an extension of the on-site leasing and marketing team, qualifying leads and booking visits.

## What it does

- Answers and replies to inbound leasing calls and emails at any hour, qualifies leads, and schedules property visits
- Catches calls and emails that would otherwise go to voicemail during or after office hours
- Engages website visitors through a click-to-chat/text widget to answer questions, book visits, or give pricing
- Routes calls, service requests, and after-hours emergencies to chosen phone numbers and devices via Answer Automation
- Takes and dispatches resident maintenance requests around the clock

## Who uses it

Property owners and operators, particularly leasing and marketing teams and maintenance staff, who want prospect and resident inquiries covered when the office is closed or staff are busy.
