# LOFT Resident Portal

LOFT Resident Portal is a resident app and portal for multifamily operators that puts leasing, moving in, everyday living, payments, and rewards under a single login. It is meant to cut manual work for onsite teams while giving residents one connected experience from application through renewal.

## What it does

- Guides applicants through application, approval, and lease signing, with identity and income verification to help catch fraud
- Helps residents move in with checklists, amenity and item reservations, and internet and utility setup
- Lets residents pay rent by card, bank account, or flexible options, with reminders and financial wellness tools
- Handles maintenance requests, updates, and community communication in one app
- Gives residents points for paying rent online, redeemable in a rewards marketplace, and lets operators fund extra incentives through Loyalty+

## Who uses it

Multifamily property operators and their onsite teams, along with the residents and applicants who use the app.
