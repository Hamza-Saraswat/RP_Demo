# Utility Submetering

A service for multifamily properties covering the sale, installation and ongoing maintenance of electric and water submeters, so residents can be billed for utilities based on their own measured usage. It also includes a maintenance program and help staying compliant with state submetering rules.

## What it does

- Installs electric and water submeters in new developments and in older properties that lack them
- Maintains existing submeter systems using a nationwide staff of in-house technicians
- Offers the Meter Assurance Plan (MAP), a tiered proactive maintenance program billed at a predictable monthly rate
- Tracks state submetering maintenance mandates and helps properties remain compliant
- Supports allocating utility costs to residents by actual consumption to recover operating expense

## Who uses it

Multifamily property managers, owners and developers who want to bill residents for actual utility use, protect their meter investment and meet state submetering requirements.
