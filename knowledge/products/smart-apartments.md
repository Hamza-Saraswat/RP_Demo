# Smart Apartments

Smart Apartments is presented through STRATIS, the intelligence layer of the RealPage Smart Building platform. It links and automates connected devices and building systems, giving operators one place to manage them and residents a mobile app to control their homes.

## What it does

- Connects to a wide range of smart devices and sensors using a hardware-agnostic approach
- Automates device behavior and workflows such as move-in/move-out routines and energy savings through rules, schedules and triggers
- Ties smart infrastructure into PMS, marketing, leasing and resident experience systems
- Gives operators a single portal with real-time visibility, alerts for leaks, low batteries and connectivity problems, and role-based permissions
- Offers a resident app for mobile-credential access, in-unit lighting and temperature control, maintenance requests, rent payments and amenity booking

## Who uses it

Property operators and on-site teams manage buildings through the portal, while residents use the mobile app for access and in-home control.
