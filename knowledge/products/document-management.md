# Document Management

OneSite Document Management is a cloud-based tool that lets multifamily operators keep forms, letters, and lease packets consistent and secure across all properties. It is built into the OneSite platform and supports electronic signing, compliance review, and bulk document handling.

## What it does

- Standardizes forms, letters, and notices so site teams use only current, corporate-approved versions
- Lets teams sign lease packets and other documents electronically, with fillable fields and signature tags for household members
- Supports internal review of affordable housing packets through a compliance analyst role, a dashboard, and activity logging
- Allows deleted documents to be restored and applies bulk actions such as printing or changing document status
- Scans and indexes documents in bulk, attaches photos to final account statements, and saves reports as a single PDF

## Who uses it

Multifamily property management companies and their onsite teams, including those handling affordable housing packet reviews.
