# Digital Disbursements

Digital Disbursements is a set of electronic payout tools for multifamily operators that replace paper checks and cash with digital methods. It covers resident refunds, utility reimbursements, on-site petty cash, and vendor payments, and it connects to Realpage accounting.

## What it does

- Sends resident refunds digitally, and residents pick a check, ACH, virtual prepaid card, or mobile wallet.
- Loads reloadable prepaid debit cards each month for utility reimbursements in affordable housing.
- Issues prepaid cards with preset limits to properties or staff in place of petty cash, and records transactions automatically in accounting.
- Automates vendor invoice processing and payment through virtual card, direct pay, or check.
- Gives real-time visibility into payments, balances, and expenses.

## Who uses it

Multifamily property management companies and their accounting, on-site, and operations staff who pay residents, vendors, and staff.
