# Vendor Payments

Vendor Payments is an accounts payable automation product that covers the procurement-to-payment cycle, from purchase order and invoice processing through to paying suppliers. It is powered by AvidXchange and integrates with the Realpage Accounting and Financial Suite.

## What it does

- Automates purchase order, invoice processing and payment workflow, shortening the cycle from about a month to about ten days
- Lets vendors be paid by Virtual Card, Direct Pay or check, with an emphasis on moving suppliers to electronic payments
- Provides payment services covering vendor conversion, execution and exception management, backed by a full-service team for onboarding and support
- Gives customizable access controls, visibility into payment status and 24/7 approval or rejection of payments to lower fraud risk
- Returns cash back on electronic payments and removes costs of printing and mailing checks

## Who uses it

Multifamily property management and real estate companies with accounts payable teams that pay many vendors.
