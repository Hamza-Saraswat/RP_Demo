# OneSite

OneSite is a property management platform for multifamily operators that brings leasing, maintenance, accounting, and daily operations into one system. It includes built-in AI assistance and supports several housing types, from conventional to senior living.

## What it does

- Handles the resident lifecycle: move-ins, move-outs, transfers, renewals, final statements, and tracking of expiring leases and delinquencies
- Gives visibility into prospects, applicants, waitlists, screening, appointments, and evictions, plus real-time views of buildings, floor plans, units, and availability
- Automates repetitive work such as month-end close, renewals, and move-outs, and uses AI to answer how-to questions, recommend vendors, and send reminders
- Offers role-based dashboards, multi-tab work across properties, and a mobile-friendly interface
- Covers maintenance with automated work orders and inventory insights, and provides regional and portfolio oversight with site-level control

## Who uses it

Multifamily owners and operators, property management companies, onsite teams, regional managers, centralized operations teams, and portfolio leaders running conventional, student, affordable, tax credit, military, or senior living properti
