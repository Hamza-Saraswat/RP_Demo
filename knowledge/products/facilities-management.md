# Facilities Management

A maintenance management platform for multifamily operators that brings work orders, inspections, compliance, and asset tracking together, with a mobile app for on-site technicians. It gives portfolio-wide visibility into maintenance activity and costs.

## What it does

- Lets technicians inspect units, create service requests, and track work orders from a mobile app that works offline and syncs automatically, with voice-to-text input
- Gives a single view of maintenance work orders across many properties, with geolocation and live updates
- Supports HUD NSPIRE compliance through inspection scheduling, documentation, and audit-ready reports
- Tracks building and unit-level assets, including warranty and lifecycle details, and connects with accounting
- Provides dashboards on response times, technician performance, costs, and compliance, plus due diligence tools for property acquisitions

## Who uses it

Multifamily property management companies and their maintenance technicians and portfolio operations teams.
