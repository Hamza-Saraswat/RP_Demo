# Knock CRM

Knock CRM is a customer relationship management platform built for multifamily property management. It brings marketing, leasing, communication and analytics into one place so teams can manage leads and move them to leases.

## What it does

- Lets leasing agents manage several properties from one dashboard, keeping leads inside the portfolio
- Automates follow-ups, tour scheduling and reminders, with AI handling chat, text, email and voice inquiries
- Tracks lead sources and marketing attribution so budget can go to the best-performing channels
- Connects with property management systems such as Realpage, Yardi and Entrata to share data and avoid duplicate entry
- Provides dashboards, real-time reporting and a gamified interface for leasing teams

## Who uses it

Multifamily property managers and their leasing teams who want to manage leads, tours and marketing performance in one system.
