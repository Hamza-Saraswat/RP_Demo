# Smart Waste Management

Smart Waste Management is a cloud-based platform that uses cameras placed inside dumpsters, along with AI, to track how full and how contaminated each container is at a multifamily property. It is built to lower hauling and contamination costs and to support sustainability reporting.

## What it does

- Watches dumpsters through installed in-container cameras and measures several performance indicators
- Sends site teams alerts several times daily about contamination and obstructions before pickup so penalties can be avoided
- Shows when containers are truly full so unnecessary pickups can be cut
- Supports data collection for compliance and sustainability reporting such as ESG/CSR, GRESB and ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager
- Provides analytics and peer benchmarking to guide waste program decisions and diversion toward recycling or composting

## Who uses it

Multifamily property owners and site teams who want to reduce waste hauling and contamination fees and report on sustainability goals.
