# Demand Generation

Demand Generation is presented on this page as DemandX, a platform that links digital advertising, leasing, and revenue management. It uses shared data and AI predictions about future vacancy to guide ad spend, lead follow-up, and pricing decisions.

## What it does

- Recommends advertising budgets and forecasts outcomes by matching spend to predicted vacancy
- Directs leasing agents to priority prospects and suggests next-best actions to speed up responses and conversions
- Supports pricing of units and amenities using AI supply and demand models
- Gives visibility into ad spend and leasing trends across multiple communities and operations functions
- Shares data and predictions across the advertising, leasing, and revenue components

## Who uses it

Property operators and multifamily teams that manage advertising, leasing, and pricing and want to reduce vacancy and improve NOI.
