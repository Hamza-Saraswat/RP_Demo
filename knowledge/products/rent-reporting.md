# Rent Reporting

Rent Reporting is an optional paid subscription for residents that sends their monthly rent payment history to credit bureaus so it can factor into their credit profiles. Communities can offer it without changing their existing processes or taking on extra administration.

## What it does

- Automatically submits each subscribing resident's monthly rent payments to at least one of the three major credit bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion)
- Bills residents monthly in advance for the subscription
- Allows every leaseholder on a unit to subscribe separately
- Covers current and future leases, but not past lease history
- Ends the subscription automatically when a lease is not renewed or the resident moves out, and continues it through a renewal

## Who uses it

Property management communities offer it to their residents, who choose to subscribe in order to build their credit profiles.
