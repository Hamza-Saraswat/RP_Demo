# Realpage Exchange (RPX)

Realpage Exchange (RPX) is Realpage's integration program that lets clients connect their Realpage data to third-party software through partners called AppPartners. Partners offer Realpage Certified integrations across many categories, and the page says contracting and onboarding for new partners have been simplified.

## What it does

- Lets clients share their Realpage data with outside software solutions
- Offers a growing network of AppPartners with Realpage Certified integrations
- Runs an AppPartner program with simplified contracting and a thorough onboarding process
- Organizes partners into categories such as collections, CRM, payments, renters insurance, and websites
- Highlights newly added AppPartners

## Who uses it

Property management clients who want to link Realpage data with third-party tools, and software vendors who want to become certified AppPartners.
