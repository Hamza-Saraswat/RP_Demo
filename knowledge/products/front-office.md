# Front Office

Front Office is a bundle of integrated, AI-enabled tools that covers the whole journey from prospect to resident. It sits alongside a property's existing PMS and aims to give operators better visibility, more efficient staffing, and smoother experiences for renters.

## What it does

- Attracts and converts leads using predictive AI, attribution, advertising, websites, listings and social/reputation tools
- Manages prospects through a CRM, centralized leasing, call intelligence and AI-driven communications
- Digitizes leasing with online leasing, contact center support, insurance, deposit alternatives and self-guided tours
- Keeps residents engaged through a resident portal, rewards, smart building features and maintenance contact center support
- Provides dashboards, KPIs and reporting, and supports a shift from community-level to regional or national operating models

## Who uses it

Property operators and leasing teams who want to streamline the prospect-to-resident journey and consolidate operations across communities.
