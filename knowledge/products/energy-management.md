# Energy Management

Energy Management is a set of services from RealPage that helps property owners lower energy and water spending. It covers buying energy, cutting consumption through audits and retrofits, checking rates and taxes, and meeting mandated benchmarking rules.

## What it does

- Sources bids from vetted suppliers and negotiates new and renewal energy contracts (natural gas, electricity, renewables) in deregulated markets
- Advises on interruptible natural gas programs, including incentive negotiation and fuel-switch notifications
- Performs on-site energy audits and manages conservation projects from proposal through measured post-project savings
- Reviews utility rates, tariffs and sales tax exemptions, and contacts providers to correct billing errors and pursue refunds
- Sets up properties in Energy Star Portfolio Manager and submits validated usage data to satisfy benchmarking ordinances

## Who uses it

Multifamily and commercial property owners and operators who want to reduce utility costs, manage energy market risk, and comply with benchmarking mandates.
