# Sustainability Services

Sustainability Services is a set of energy reporting, management, and conservation tools plus professional services for multifamily owners, investors, and operators. It draws on a large utility expense database to help portfolios cut utility costs, meet reporting requirements, and raise property value.

## What it does

- Collects and centralizes ESG data, tracks portfolio performance, and supports GRESB and CDP submissions, with alerts on abnormal consumption
- Handles mandatory energy and water benchmarking through the EPA's Portfolio Manager, including data gathering, validation, and upload
- Reviews utility invoices, rates, and tariffs to catch billing errors and recover sales tax exemptions and refunds
- Negotiates electric, gas, and renewable energy contracts and manages interruptible natural gas
- Runs on-site audits and manages conservation projects, from proposals through ongoing performance reporting

## Who uses it

Multifamily property owners, investors, and operators who want to control energy and water spend, comply with benchmarking and building performance rules, and report on ESG.
