# Deposit Alternatives

Deposit Alternatives is a set of options that let property operators swap traditional upfront security deposits for lower-cost move-in choices. It aims to protect against unpaid rent and damages while helping communities lease without losing prospects to high deposits.

## What it does

- Replaces upfront security deposits with a one-time, non-refundable surety bond through DepositIQ
- Replaces cash deposits with a non-insurance option through Obligo, with quick ACH payouts at move-out
- Lets communities advertise lower move-in costs to draw more traffic and lease faster
- Helps reduce bad debt and the effort of administering deposits, claims, and collections
- Shares data and a consistent experience through integration with OneSite

## Who uses it

Property management companies and apartment community operators, including their marketing, leasing, and finance teams, along with the residents who pay lower move-in costs.
