# Loyalty Rewards

Loyalty Rewards is a resident retention program tied to the LOFT platform that gives residents points for paying rent online and lets property teams send digital incentives. It comes in a free tier, a paid tier with customizable client-funded rewards, and a standalone option for properties that don't use LOFT.

## What it does

- Awards residents points automatically each time they pay rent online, with the points funded by Realpage
- Lets authorized staff send and track digital concessions such as move-in gifts, thank-yous and renewal offers, and recovers funds that go unused
- Gives residents a digital marketplace of 100+ brands where they can browse and redeem rewards themselves
- Adds home service credits and travel credits through partners in the free tier
- Lets the paid Loyalty+ tier reward actions like referrals, reviews, renewals and surveys using points the client funds

## Who uses it

Property managers and owners who want to improve resident retention and on-time rent payment, including third-party managers who can use the standalone Community Rewards app without LOFT.
