# Apartment Tours

Apartment Tours is a tablet-based touring platform for leasing teams that supports the whole prospect visit, from scheduling through follow-up. It also includes ID-scanning and identity checks intended to reduce rental fraud. The page refers to it as Knock Tours.

## What it does

- Scans IDs through a scanner tied to the guest card and confirms them against a large set of documents from many countries
- Lets prospects confirm who they are quickly with a selfie scan
- Gives leasing staff a dashboard of the day's tour schedule and an itinerary with key property details
- Shows interactive property and neighborhood maps, digital floor plans, and photos during the visit
- Lets staff send a quote at the end of a tour with one click, and offers video, live-agent, or self-guided tour formats

## Who uses it

Leasing teams at multifamily communities use it to run tours, while management uses the resulting data to improve the sales process.
