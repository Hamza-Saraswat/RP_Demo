# Insurance

Realpage Insurance is a set of customizable insurance and security deposit alternative programs for multifamily and single-family owners and operators. It covers resident coverage, master policies, and bond-based deposit options, with the program administration handled for the property team.

## What it does

- Structures and manages renters insurance, master insurance, and deposit alternative programs tailored to each community
- Offers RealCoverage, which pairs resident coverage with a master policy and automates certificate tracking and resident communication
- Provides eRenterPlan with RenterProtection gap coverage so vacant units and uninsured residents are still covered
- Uses AssetProtect to insure resident-caused damage under one master policy, and Policy Validator to check third-party policies
- Offers DepositIQ, a one-time surety bond residents can buy in place of a traditional security deposit

## Who uses it

Owners and operators of multifamily and single-family properties, along with their on-site staff and residents.
