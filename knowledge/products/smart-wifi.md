# Smart WiFi

WhiteSky is a fully managed, gigabit-class Wi-Fi and internet service for apartment communities, run on the provider's own nationwide network. It links residents, guests, staff and IoT devices, and gives operators a way to earn ancillary income.

## What it does

- Provides managed property-wide Wi-Fi that connects residents, guests, staff and IoT devices
- Serves as the network backbone for connected building devices and Smart Apartments
- Runs on an owned nationwide ISP network with 20+ points of presence, proactive monitoring and 24/7 tiered support
- Lets operators earn ancillary revenue and recoup the investment while keeping ownership of the infrastructure
- Integrates with STRATIS for device provisioning and access control, and sends network data to Lumina AI for analytics

## Who uses it

Multifamily property operators and owners who want managed Wi-Fi for residents and a network foundation for smart building systems.
