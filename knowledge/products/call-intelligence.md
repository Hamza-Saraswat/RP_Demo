# Call Intelligence

Call Intelligence records and analyzes phone conversations between prospects and leasing staff, turning them into reports on what callers want and how agents perform. It is built into Knock CRM so call insights flow straight into prospect records.

## What it does

- Captures call data and reports on prospect preferences
- Supplements secret phone shops and reduces manual listening to recordings
- Offers drill-down links and keyword search by property, agent, and call
- Gives leaders insight to coach agents and convert more calls into visits
- Feeds call insights into Knock CRM so prospect information stays current

## Who uses it

Multifamily leasing teams, property managers, and training or performance leaders who want to monitor and improve how phone leads are handled.
