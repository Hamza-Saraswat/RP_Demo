# Market Analytics

Market Analytics is a web and mobile intelligence platform that gives multifamily, student housing, and build-to-rent stakeholders visibility into market, submarket, and property performance. It combines proprietary lease data, daily property updates, sales data, and AI-driven forecasts to support investment and operating decisions.

## What it does

- Tracks rents, concessions, floor plans, availability, and occupancy with daily property-level updates
- Produces 5-year forecasts for markets, submarkets, and individual assets using AI-powered econometric modeling
- Provides Asset Intelligence for automated property valuations, sales and rent comps, and configurable assumptions
- Offers student housing metrics for nearly 1,000 universities, including radius-based forecasting
- Delivers custom feasibility studies from in-house economists and analysts, plus data extracts, reports, and a mobile app

## Who uses it

Property managers, owners, investors, developers, lenders, and brokers working in conventional, student, and build-to-rent multifamily housing.
