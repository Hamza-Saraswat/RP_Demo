# Vendor Management

Vendor Management is a suite for handling vendor relationships from finding suppliers through paying them. It aims to help property companies choose vendors, lower operating costs, and limit risk to their properties.

## What it does

- Lets property management companies search for and connect with vendors by category, location, and other criteria
- Checks vendors against insurance, licensing, and background check requirements the customer defines before they work on site
- Sends reminders about upcoming insurance policy expirations and weekly notices of non-compliance
- Provides group purchasing discounts with national and regional vendors, plus reporting and analytics on purchasing
- Centralizes the source-to-pay process, covering orders, receipt of goods, invoice matching, and payment through Realpage Source to Pay

## Who uses it

Property management companies and the vendors they work with, including residential operators and third-party managers who want to control vendor cost and liability risk.
