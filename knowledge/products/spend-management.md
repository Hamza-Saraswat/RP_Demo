# Spend Management

Spend Management is a software suite for multifamily operators that brings purchasing, invoicing, and vendor oversight into one automated, centralized workflow. It gives teams live visibility into spending against budget, with Lumina-powered assistance available.

## What it does

- Automates purchase orders and approvals across a portfolio, with real-time comparison of spend to budget
- Speeds up invoice processing and makes it more accurate and timely
- Supports vendor management, including risk reduction, payment handling, and discount negotiation
- Offers a mobile app for approving purchases on the go
- Includes an AI copilot in the vendor storefront that answers how-to questions, opens support cases, connects users to live experts, and points to guides

## Who uses it

Multifamily operators and management companies, including purchasing, operations, and finance staff as well as their suppliers and vendors.
