# Websites

Websites is a property website offering built so that AI tools can read and cite the content, while also guiding visiting renters toward signing a lease. It is delivered as part of the Demand Marketing solution and includes creative services for branding and design.

## What it does

- Builds mobile-first property sites with content structured so AI can read, cite, and recommend the community
- Provides AI-powered interactive 3D property maps showing layout, nearby points of interest, and amenities
- Displays live pricing and availability through integration with major property management systems, with fee transparency
- Meets current WCAG accessibility requirements at launch
- Includes technical and on-page SEO such as title tags, meta descriptions, speed optimization, keyword strategy, Search Console setup, and recurring health audits

## Who uses it

Multifamily property operators who want their communities to be found by AI search tools and to convert renter visits into leases.
