# AI Revenue Management

AI Revenue Management is a Realpage tool that gives multifamily owners and operators daily apartment pricing recommendations meant to balance supply and demand against their asset strategy. It works with most property management systems and comes with advisory support and configurable compliance settings.

## What it does

- Analyzes market and property data and recommends apartment pricing automatically each day
- Optimizes renewal pricing according to the customer's chosen strategy
- Supports lease-ups, concessions strategies, renovations, and small-unit pricing changes
- Lets users review and make the final decision on pricing recommendations, with transparent logic
- Connects to most major property management systems and can be configured to meet federal, state, and local requirements

## Who uses it

Multifamily revenue managers, leasing teams, owners, and asset managers who set and oversee apartment pricing.
