# AI Screening

AI Screening is a Realpage resident screening product that uses a machine-learning predictive score to judge how likely an applicant is to prioritize paying rent, going beyond a credit-based view of ability to pay. It is aimed at lowering bad debt and financial loss for multifamily operators.

## What it does

- Produces a predictive applicant score built with AI and machine learning for multifamily use
- Draws on a proprietary rental history database of more than 30 million lease outcome records
- Fits into the rental application and leasing workflow regardless of the property management system in use
- Aims to cut bad debt, skips, and evictions by choosing more suitable residents
- Supplies related content on rental application fraud, including a 2024 research study whitepaper

## Who uses it

Multifamily property management companies and on-site leasing teams that screen rental applicants and want to manage risk.
