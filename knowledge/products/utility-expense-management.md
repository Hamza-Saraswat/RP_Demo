# Utility Expense Management

Utility Expense Management is a RealPage service that captures, tracks, and pays property utility invoices while watching usage and rates. It aims to cut avoidable charges such as late fees and unrecovered costs, and gives reporting on savings.

## What it does

- Captures invoice totals plus line items and metrics such as effective rate and usage
- Pays invoices faster and monitors for missing bills to reduce late fees and shut-offs
- Sends alerts on unusual water, gas, and electric consumption
- Recovers utility costs that were not properly passed on to residents
- Provides benchmarking, analytics and reporting, and rate forecasting to support budgets

## Who uses it

Property owners and operators, such as multifamily management firms, who want to control utility operating costs across their properties.
