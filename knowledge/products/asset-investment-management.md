# Asset Investment Management

RealPage AIM is a platform that pulls together asset-level and portfolio-level financial and operating data from many sources, cleans and standardizes it, and presents it through reports and dashboards. It is meant to help asset and portfolio managers find opportunities, track performance, and manage risk.

## What it does

- Collects data from multiple sources, formats, and asset classes, with automated ingestion plus AI-driven normalization and validation
- Standardizes financial data so KPIs can be compared across investments and stronger or weaker assets are easier to spot
- Scores data quality on consistency, completeness, and timeliness through an AI dashboard, supported by governance reviews
- Offers reporting and analysis tools including Portfolio Explorer (drag and drop), an Excel Add-In, self-service Power BI, and configurable dashboards
- Provides an API for extracting normalized data for consolidation and further analysis

## Who uses it

Asset managers and portfolio managers in commercial and residential real estate who oversee investments and report to investors and stakeholders.
