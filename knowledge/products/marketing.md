# Marketing

Demand Marketing is an end-to-end digital marketing offering for multifamily operators, aimed at helping properties show up in search and AI-assisted discovery, attract renters ready to lease, and tie marketing activity to lease results.

## What it does

- Builds apartment websites that AI systems can read and cite, with mobile-first design, accessibility, fee transparency, and interactive 3D property maps
- Coordinates SEO, GEO, and AEO under Agentic Search Optimization to keep properties discoverable
- Runs paid advertising that uses AI Call Scoring to spot callers likely to rent and refine campaign targeting
- Handles listings across 50+ directories, Google Business Profile, and review requests and replies, with sentiment and competitor reporting
- Reports on how sources, activities, and spend relate to visits, applications, and leases, and offers in-house creative services such as branding, copywriting, and design

## Who uses it

Multifamily property managers and apartment operators who want to improve online visibility and see which marketing efforts lead to leases.
