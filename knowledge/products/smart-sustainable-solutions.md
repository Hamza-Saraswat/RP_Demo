# Smart Sustainable Solutions

Smart Sustainable Solutions is a set of products that pairs submeter technology with AI so multifamily properties can lower the cost of handling waste and water in shared areas and individual units. It also positions the properties as sustainable-living buildings, which the page links to resident satisfaction and retention.

## What it does

- Uses AI and a rugged camera mounted on each dumpster to monitor waste and recycling operations (Smart Waste)
- Reports per-unit waste costs, contamination fees, and recycling diversion ratios at property and portfolio level
- Sends actionable alerts and gives more visibility into vendor waste services
- Uses IoT submeters to track water use in common areas and residential units (Smart Water)
- Detects leaks and consumption problems and notifies site teams, including in areas like irrigation and pools that usually lack meters

## Who uses it

Multifamily property owners and operators, and their site teams and portfolio managers, who want to cut waste and water costs.
