# Lumina Ascent

Lumina Ascent is a portfolio intelligence platform for multifamily that converts property, asset and market data into simple performance signals with visible supporting drivers. It points teams toward the opportunities most worth acting on.

## What it does

- Condenses large volumes of property and market metrics into easy-to-read performance signals for assets and portfolios
- Shows the metrics behind each signal so teams can see what is driving a change
- Separates effects of market conditions from those of operational execution
- Ranks assets, KPIs and actions by potential impact so teams know where to focus
- Lets users switch between portfolio-wide, asset-level and market views

## Who uses it

Owners and executives, asset managers, portfolio and regional leaders, property operations teams, and marketing, finance and acquisition teams responsible for multifamily portfolio performance.
