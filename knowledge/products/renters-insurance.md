# Renters Insurance

A renters insurance offering for multifamily housing that lets operators and residents handle coverage inside their existing property management and resident tools. It centers on the eRenterPlan program, with an optional gap policy for units or residents left uncovered.

## What it does

- Gives residents renters insurance coverage through eRenterPlan, built for multifamily
- Offers optional RenterProtection gap coverage for vacant units or residents without insurance
- Tracks policy status and updates compliance records automatically
- Flags residents who are not compliant with the insurance requirement
- Builds insurance enrollment into the leasing process and shows compliance data in one platform

## Who uses it

Multifamily property managers and owners who require renters insurance, and the residents who buy coverage.
