# Smart Digital Advertising

Smart Digital Advertising is a paid-media service for multifamily properties that runs ads across search, social, display and remarketing. It uses AI scoring of phone callers and daily budget adjustments to aim spend at renters likely to sign leases.

## What it does

- Runs paid search, performance max, paid social and remarketing ads tailored to apartment marketing
- Scores incoming callers for intent to rent so ad targeting favors higher-quality leads
- Shifts budget daily toward the best-performing campaigns using multi-touch attribution
- Tracks leads, clicks and calls in dashboards and ties ad results to occupancy
- Connects with PMS, CRM and website systems to reflect current pricing and availability

## Who uses it

Multifamily property management companies and marketers, including those with single family rental (BTR) and student housing properties.
