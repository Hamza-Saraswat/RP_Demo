# Resident Screening

Resident Screening is a suite of applicant vetting tools that pulls together credit, criminal, eviction and rental payment history data, plus identity and income checks, to give leasing teams a risk assessment for each rental application. It works as decision support inside a property's existing leasing process rather than making decisions on its own.

## What it does

- Runs credit checks (using the three major bureaus), criminal and sex offender searches, and eviction and collections reports
- Draws on a large database of actual rental payment records from over 20,000 reporting communities
- Confirms applicant identity through several methods and verifies income via bank, payroll or documents
- Uses AI to spot suspicious patterns that may indicate fraud and to produce a risk score based on the client's own leasing criteria
- Feeds recommendations into existing leasing systems and offers portfolio-wide reporting

## Who uses it

Property management companies and on-site leasing teams, including operators of large portfolios, who need to evaluate rental applicants and reduce fraud and bad debt.
