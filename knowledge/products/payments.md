# Payments

Payments is an online rent payment offering that lets residents pay rent, application fees, and deposits through a central portal using several methods. It ties into property management systems so payments post to the ledger automatically and staff handle less paper.

## What it does

- Accepts rent, application fee, and deposit payments online and on mobile via bank transfer (ACH), credit card, or debit card
- Lets residents split rent into smaller installments through Livble
- Converts paper payments into digital ones through cash payments at retail locations, a lockbox service, and check scanning
- Posts payments to the ledger automatically by integrating with systems such as Yardi, MRI, and OneSite Leasing and Rents
- Builds in compliance and security measures and removes checks and money orders from the office to lower fraud risk

## Who uses it

Multifamily property management companies and their on-site staff, along with the residents who pay rent through the portal.
