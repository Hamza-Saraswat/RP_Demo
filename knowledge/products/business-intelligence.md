# Business Intelligence

Business Intelligence is an analytics product from Realpage that pulls together property data from across a multifamily portfolio and refreshes it daily. It gives owners and operators dashboards, reports, and scorecards to spot risks and opportunities.

## What it does

- Connects to all major operating systems and consolidates portfolio data with daily refreshes
- Provides customizable dashboards, foundational reports, scorecards, and a custom report writer
- Offers drill-down to transaction-level detail and blends in external data
- Tracks a wide range of KPIs across operations, marketing, demographics, facilities, screening, accounting, and affordable compliance
- Supports conventional, student, senior, and affordable properties

## Who uses it

Multifamily owners, portfolio managers, and regional managers who need a portfolio-wide view of property performance.
